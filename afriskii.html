FAQs

Common Questions

What is the Makena Snow Weekend?

The Makena Snow Weekend is our full five-day winter experience at Camelback Resort in Tannersville, Pennsylvania — February 4–8, 2027. Think pool parties, bonfire nights, après vibes, and Sunday group dinner. No skiing required. This is the Makena energy, mountain edition.

What's included in the Apres Ski All Access pass?

The Apres Ski All Access ($255) covers every event across the weekend — Rich All White Party, Indoor Pool Party, Bonfire Party, Après Party on both Friday and Saturday nights — plus the full Makena atmosphere. No accommodation included. No skiing or snowtubing included. This is strictly the après life.

Does the All Access pass include skiing or snowtubing?

No — and that's intentional. The Apres Ski All Access is about the events, the energy, and the culture. If you want to hit the slopes, add a Ski & Snowboard Day Pass separately for $275.

What's the difference between Stay Slay and Makena Snow Escape?

Stay Slay ($850/person) includes a shared king room — you'll share the room with another guest. Makena Snow Escape ($1,200/person) gives you a fully private room to yourself. Both include the same events, accommodation for 4 nights (Feb 4–8), and the full Makena experience.

What is the deposit and when is the balance due?

A $200 non-refundable deposit secures your spot today. The remaining balance (Stay Slay: ~$650 · Snow Escape: ~$1,000) is due by January 1, 2027. Makena will reach out with payment details closer to the date.

What is the age requirement?

21+ only. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for entry to all events.
