Questions

What happens after I apply?
We review your application and reach out personally within 2–3 business days. If it's a good fit, we'll have a quick conversation to discuss how the program works, what you can earn, and get you set up with your discount code.
Do I need to be in DC to apply?
No. You can be based anywhere — if your network is buying tickets to Makena events, your code works. That said, local DC ambassadors get extra perks like free entry and free +1s they can actually use in person.
Do I need a large social media following?
Not at all. We care about your community and how you engage with it, not your follower count. Some of the best ambassadors sell tickets purely through their personal network — no big platform needed.
How are sales tracked?
Every ticket purchase made using your personal code is tracked automatically. You'll get a clear breakdown of your sales and earnings — no guesswork involved.
