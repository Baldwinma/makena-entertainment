FAQs

Common Questions

What is Afriskii Fest?

Afriskii Fest is an annual winter getaway that brings together Black culture lovers for a full resort weekend of parties, pool events, and unforgettable nights. The 2027 event is at Camelback Resort in Tannersville, Pennsylvania — February 4–7. Makena has partnered with Afriskii to bring our own curated events to the weekend.

Do I need to buy Afriskii tickets separately?

For the Afriskii Fest admission and main resort access, you'll need to book through Afriskii directly. The Makena All Access Pass covers our signature All White Affair plus all Afriskii-hosted events (pool party, bonfire, both après ski nights). The Makena Snow Escape is the only fully all-in-one option — accommodation included.

What's included in the All Access Pass?

The All Access Pass (from $255) covers Makena's signature All White Affair on Saturday, plus the Afriskii-hosted Indoor Pool Party, Bonfire Party, and Après Ski parties on both Friday and Saturday nights — along with the full Makena atmosphere across all four days.

What's the All White Affair dress code?

All white, no exceptions. Head-to-toe white attire is required for entry to the All White Affair on Saturday, February 7. This is the Makena standard — we keep it tight.

What's the age requirement?

21+ only. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for entry to all events.

When does the Makena Snow Escape go on sale?

Pricing and dates are being finalized. Drop your email in the interest form above and you'll be the first to know when bookings open. Spots will be limited — early interest gets early access.
