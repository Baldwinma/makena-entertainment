How the Week Looks

5 days, zero planning required on your end.

  • Day1

    Arrival + Welcome Mixer + All White Pool Party

    Private airport transfers, hotel check-in, welcome gift bag, welcome drinks — then kick the trip off right with an all white pool party.

  • Day2

    Boat Party + Group Dinner

    Full-day boat party — open bar, DJ, music and vibes on the water. Evening: private group dinner with fun activities.

  • Day3

    Pink Pool Party + Interactive Games

    Daytime pink pool party with drinks and good energy. Evening: fun interactive group games and activities.

  • Day4

    Adventure Day + Neon Pool Party

    Morning adrenaline — dune buggies and zip-lining through the DR. Finish it all off with a neon pool party at night.

  • Day5

    Group Breakfast + Goodbye Drinks

    One last morning together — group breakfast, goodbye drinks, group photo, then private transfers back to the airport.
